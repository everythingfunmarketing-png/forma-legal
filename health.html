Health notice and sources

Forma · Last updated October 2026

Estimates only

Forma's calorie targets, macros, photo suggestions and recovery scores are estimates to help you plan. They are not medical advice, and individual needs vary.

Calorie targets

Maintenance calories are estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, or the Katch-McArdle equation if you enter body fat. Targets for losing fat, building muscle or both apply a modest adjustment, with a minimum calorie floor and a cap on how fast weight is expected to change. After several weeks of logs, Forma compares your intake with your weight trend to refine the estimate.

Protein

Protein targets of about 1.6 to 2.2 grams per kilogram of body weight follow the range commonly cited for people who train with weights.

Recovery

Recovery scores are Forma's own estimates. They combine recent training volume, time since each muscle was trained, and the sleep, stress and soreness you report. They are not a clinical measure.

Strength

Estimated one-rep max uses the Epley formula, and is a guide only.

Photo scanning

Photo suggestions come from on-device models and can be wrong, especially for portion sizes. Check the food and the amount before you log.
