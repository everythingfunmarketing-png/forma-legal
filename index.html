Privacy

Private by design.

Your health data is yours. Forma is built so you stay in control of it.

  • Your data stays on your phone unless you sign in to back it up.
  • No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.
  • Meal photos are only sent for AI analysis after you agree, are used just to identify the food, and are never stored.
  • Export everything as a spreadsheet, or delete your account, at any time.
