Privacy

Private by design.

Your health data is yours. Forma is built so you stay in control of it.

  • Your data stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up with Sign in with Apple.
  • No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.
  • Meal photos are analysed on your phone and never uploaded.
  • Export everything as a spreadsheet, or delete your account, at any time.
