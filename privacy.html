Privacy Policy

Forma · Last updated October 2026

Summary

Forma keeps your data on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Apple, Forma also backs it up to a private cloud account so you can use it on more than one device. Forma has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and it never sells your data.

What Forma stores

Your profile (sex, birth year, height, weight, activity level, goal and training preferences), the food you log, your workouts and sets, your daily check-ins, your weigh-ins, foods you create, and your settings. This is health and fitness information, and you decide what to enter.

Where it lives

On your device, in the app's own storage. If you sign in, a copy is stored in our cloud database, provided by Supabase, under your account. Each person's records are separated by database rules so that only that signed-in account can read or change them.

Signing in with Apple

Sign in with Apple is optional. If you use it, we receive an identifier for your account from Apple, and your email address if you choose to share it (Apple may provide a private relay address). Your name, if provided, stays on your device. Signing in is only needed for cloud backup and sync. Everything else works without an account.

Photos and camera

When you scan a meal, the photo is analysed on your device by Apple's on-device models. It is not uploaded, not saved by Forma and not shared. Forma only reads a photo you take or choose.

Food search

When you search for a food, the words you type are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), an open food database, to find matching products. No account details are sent with the search. Open Food Facts can see your network address, as any website can.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server, and you can turn each one off in Settings.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across other apps or sites, no analytics, no selling or renting of your data, and no use of your data to train AI models.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Settings has Erase all data, which removes everything on the phone, and Delete account, which removes your account and all of your cloud data. Deleting the app removes the data on the phone. Deletions in the cloud are permanent.

Children

Forma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data can be sent to the support contact listed on Forma's App Store page.
