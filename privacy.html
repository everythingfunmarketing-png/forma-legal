Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Summary

Forma keeps your data on your phone. If you sign in, it also backs your data up to a private cloud account so you can use it on more than one device. Forma has no ads and no tracking, and it never sells your data. It counts a few anonymous app steps, such as finishing setup, to improve the app.

What Forma stores

Your profile (sex, birth year, height, weight, activity level, goal and training preferences), your name if you enter it, the food you log, saved meals and favourites, your workouts and sets, any training plan you enter, your daily check-ins, your weigh-ins, foods you create, and your settings. This is health and fitness information, and you decide what to enter.

Where it lives

On your device, in the app's own storage. If you sign in, a copy is stored in our cloud database, provided by Supabase, under your account. Each person's records are separated by database rules so that only that signed-in account can read or change them.

Signing in

Signing in is optional for logging and training. You can sign in with Apple or with an email address and password. With Apple, we receive an identifier for your account and your email address if you choose to share it (Apple may provide a private relay address). With email, we store your email address and a securely hashed password with our provider, Supabase. An account is needed for cloud backup, the AI meal scan and the leaderboard.

AI meal scan

Before your first AI scan, Forma asks for your permission. When you scan a meal, the photo, any words you add about it, and the distance measured by your phone's depth sensor are sent over an encrypted connection to Forma's server and on to Anthropic's Claude AI, to identify the foods and estimate amounts. They are used only for that analysis and are not stored by Forma or used to train AI models. Forma keeps a count of your scans and their cost to run your scan allowance. You can always log food by search, barcode or by hand instead.

Anonymous app statistics

To see where people get stuck and to improve Forma, the app sends which setup and subscription steps a phone reaches (for example finishing setup, seeing the subscription screen, starting a free trial or logging a first meal), the yearly price that phone was shown, your answer to “Where did you hear about Forma?” and any creator code you type. Each step is sent once, with a random number made by the app. It is not your advertising identifier and is not linked to your account, name, email or anything you log. These counts are kept by Forma, used only to improve the app, and never shared or sold.

Food search and barcodes

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the words or barcode number are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), an open food database, to find matching products. No account details are sent. Open Food Facts can see your network address, as any website can. When a food isn't in Forma's built-in lists, the words you typed (not your account or anything else) are also sent to Forma's server and on to Anthropic's Claude AI to look up the food's standard values. Answers are saved by the food's name so everyone gets the same numbers.

Reporting wrong values

If you tap Report wrong values on a food, its name, the values shown, the amount and the problem you picked are sent to Forma with the same random app number used for app statistics, so the food can be checked. Nothing about your account is sent.

Apple Health

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, Forma saves your meals and macros, weigh-ins and workouts to Apple Health, and reads weigh-ins, sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, active calories and workouts from it, including data that WHOOP, Strava and other wearables and apps save there. Sleep, heart, activity and workout readings are shown on your phone only and are not uploaded. Health data is only used to show your own progress in Forma. It is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else, and never sold. You can turn this off at any time in Settings or in the Health app.

Progress photos

Progress photos you add are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded or backed up by Forma, and Erase all data in Settings deletes them.

Leaderboard

The streak leaderboard is optional and only for accounts signed in with Apple. If you join, your first name and last initial and your streak length are shown to other Forma users. You can leave at any time, which removes you from the board. Names can be reported, and offensive names are removed.

Purchases

Subscriptions and Scan Packs are bought through Apple. Forma never sees your payment details. To give you the scans you paid for, Forma's server checks Apple's signed record of your purchase.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone from what you log there. Nothing about you is sent to a notification server, and you can turn each reminder off in Settings.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across other apps or sites, no third-party analytics or advertising tools, no selling or renting of your data, and no use of your data to train AI models.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Settings has Export my data, which gives you a spreadsheet of everything you logged, Erase all data, which removes everything on the phone, and Delete account, which removes your account, your cloud data, your leaderboard entry and your scan records. Deleting the app removes the data on the phone. Deletions in the cloud are permanent.

Children

Forma is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app with a new date.
