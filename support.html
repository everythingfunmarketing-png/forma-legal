Common questions

Is Forma free?

Forma is free during early access, including photo scanning. If paid features are added later, we will say so clearly in the app.

How accurate are the calories and macros?

They are estimates to help you plan. Food values come from built-in averages and an open food database, and portion sizes depend on what you enter. Check the amount before you log. Forma is not medical advice.

Which iPhones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 18 or later. Scanning a meal with the camera uses Apple's on-device model, which needs iOS 27 and a phone that supports Apple Intelligence. Other phones use basic on-device recognition, and food search works everywhere.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Sign in with Apple is optional and only used to back up your data and sync it between devices.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Open Settings in the app. Choose Delete account to remove your account and all cloud data, and Erase all data to remove everything stored on your phone. Deleting the app also removes the data on the phone.

How do I export my data?

In the app, open Settings, then Setup, then Export my data. You get a spreadsheet of your food, weight, workouts and check-ins.

How does Forma suggest changes to my own workout plan?

Add your routine on the Train tab, then choose Check my plan. Forma compares it with each muscle's recovery time and with common starting-point guidelines for muscle growth: about 10 to 20 hard sets per muscle a week, each muscle trained about twice a week, enough time between sessions, and balanced pushing and pulling. You see why behind each suggestion and choose whether to apply it. They are guidelines for healthy adults, not medical advice.

Where can I learn how everything works?

Open Settings, then How Forma works. The guide explains recovery scores, plan checks, calories and macros, progress and your data in plain words. It is also shown once after you finish setup.

Why does Forma set maintenance calories for me?

Forma does not set calorie deficits for people under 18 or whose weight is low for their height. Growing bodies and underweight people need the energy. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before cutting calories.

Where does the food information come from?

A set of built-in average values, plus Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), an open database. The text of a food search is sent to Open Food Facts to find matches. See the Privacy Policy for details.

A food is missing or wrong. What can I do?

Use Create food in the Add food screen to enter it from the label. It is saved to My foods so you can log it again in one tap.
